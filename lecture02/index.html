本讲切入点Today's Entry Point

经典波有波动方程（弦振动、电磁波）。如果粒子真的是波，那量子“物质波”的方程是什么？Classical waves have wave equations. If particles are really waves, then what is the equation of a quantum matter wave?

起点Starting Point

自由粒子的平面波The Plane Wave of a Free Particle

最简单的波The Simplest Wave

\psi(x,t) = A e^{i(kx-\omega t)}

为什么从这里出发？Why start here?

它是最标准的行波形式，学生在电磁学和振动中已经见过类似结构。It is the standard traveling-wave form already familiar from electromagnetism and vibrations.

关键点Key Point

量子波函数天然是复数波，而不是经典实数振幅。A quantum wavefunction is naturally a complex wave, not a classical real amplitude.

从理想到现实From Ideal to Real

平面波的问题：粒子在哪里？The Problem with Plane Waves: Where Is the Particle?

平面波完全非局域Plane Waves Are Completely Delocalized

单一平面波 e^{i(kx-\omega t)} 的 |\psi|^2 = |A|^2 处处相同——粒子在空间中均匀分布，找不到"位置"。A single plane wave e^{i(kx-\omega t)} has |\psi|^2 = |A|^2 everywhere — the particle is uniformly distributed, with no position at all.

解决方案：叠加不同波数Solution: Superpose Different Wave Numbers

\psi(x,t) = \int A(k)\, e^{i(kx - \omega(k)t)}\, dk

不同 k 的平面波叠加 → 波包：在某处集中，其余地方几乎为零。Superposing plane waves of different k → a wave packet: concentrated somewhere, nearly zero elsewhere.

群速度 = 经典速度Group Velocity = Classical Velocity

v_g = \frac{d\omega}{dk} = \frac{\hbar k}{m} = \frac{p}{m}

波包中心以经典粒子速度运动——量子力学在宏观极限下回归经典。The packet center moves at the classical particle velocity — quantum mechanics recovers classical behavior in the macroscopic limit.

色散与展宽Dispersion and Spreading

量子物质波有色散（\omega \propto k^2），因此波包会随时间展宽。局域化越好（\Delta x 越小），动量展宽越大（\Delta p 越大）。Quantum matter waves are dispersive (\omega \propto k^2), so packets spread over time. Better localization (smaller \Delta x) means larger momentum spread (\Delta p).

不确定关系的预告Preview of the Uncertainty Relation

\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \geq \hbar/2：局域化与动量精度不可兼得，这不是测量缺陷，而是波的本质。\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \geq \hbar/2: localization and momentum precision cannot coexist — not a measurement flaw, but the nature of waves.

翻译字典Translation Dictionary

把量子关系翻译成波动语言Translate Quantum Relations into Wave Language

经典波Classical Wave

  • 波数与频率由介质性质决定。k and \omega come from the medium.
  • 典型色散：\omega = c kTypical dispersion: \omega = ck.
  • 相速度与群速度常常相同。Phase and group velocity are often the same.

量子物质波Quantum Matter Wave

  • p = \hbar k：动量 ↔ 波数p = \hbar k: momentum ↔ wave number.
  • E = \hbar \omega：能量 ↔ 角频率E = \hbar \omega: energy ↔ angular frequency.
  • 自由粒子：E = p^2/2m \Rightarrow \omega = \hbar k^2/2mFree particle: E = p^2/2m \Rightarrow \omega = \hbar k^2/2m.
重要区别Important Difference

量子物质波具有色散，和经典电磁波的线性色散关系不同。Matter waves are dispersive. Their dispersion differs fundamentally from the linear classical relation \omega = ck.

猜方程 · 时间Guessing the Equation · Time

对时间求导，提取出能量Differentiate in Time to Extract Energy

\psi = e^{i(kx-\omega t)}

\frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t} = -i\omega \psi

乘以 i\hbar：Multiply by i\hbar:

i\hbar \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t} = \hbar \omega \psi

再用 E = \hbar \omega：Now use E = \hbar \omega:

i\hbar \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t} = E \psi

这一步意味着什么？What does this mean?

时间导数对应了“能量”这一物理量，因此 i\hbar \partial/\partial t 会成为能量算符的候选形式。The time derivative extracts the quantity we interpret as energy, so i\hbar \partial/\partial t becomes the candidate energy operator.

猜方程 · 空间Guessing the Equation · Space

对空间求二阶导，提取出动能Differentiate in Space to Extract Kinetic Energy

\frac{\partial^2 \psi}{\partial x^2} = -k^2 \psi

乘以 -\hbar^2：Multiply by -\hbar^2:

-\hbar^2 \frac{\partial^2 \psi}{\partial x^2} = \hbar^2 k^2 \psi

再用 p = \hbar k：Then use p = \hbar k:

-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2 \psi}{\partial x^2} = \frac{p^2}{2m}\psi

结论Conclusion

空间二阶导自然对应自由粒子的动能项，因此 -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2} 成为动能算符。The second spatial derivative naturally corresponds to kinetic energy, giving the operator -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}.

核心结果Core Result

把两部分拼起来：薛定谔方程Put the Pieces Together: The Schrödinger Equation

自由粒子Free Particle

E = \frac{p^2}{2m}

i\hbar \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t} = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2 \psi}{\partial x^2}

加入势能Add Potential Energy

i\hbar \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t} = \left[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2} + V(x)\right]\psi

注意措辞Important Wording

我们不是严格“推导”了这个方程，而是从 de Broglie 关系和最简单平面波出发，合理地猜出了它；它的正确性由实验验证。We did not strictly derive this equation. We guessed it in a physically motivated way from de Broglie relations and the simplest plane wave, and experiment validates it.

波函数的含义Meaning of the Wavefunction

\psi(x,t) 到底是什么？What Does \psi(x,t) Mean?

Born 概率诠释Born's Probability Interpretation

|\psi(x,t)|^2 dx

表示在位置 x 附近宽度 dx 的区间内找到粒子的概率。gives the probability of finding the particle in an interval dx near position x.

\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} |\psi|^2 dx = 1

归一化意味着“粒子一定在某处”。Normalization means the particle must be somewhere.

从复数到概率From Complex Amplitude to Probability

\psi 通常是复数，本身不是可直接测量量；真正变成概率的是 |\psi|^2。\psi is generally complex and not directly observable; what becomes physically meaningful as probability is |\psi|^2.

概率守恒Probability Conservation

薛定谔方程保证 \frac{d}{dt}\int|\psi|^2 dx = 0：如果波函数一开始归一化，它将永远归一化。概率不会凭空产生或消失。The Schrödinger equation guarantees \frac{d}{dt}\int|\psi|^2 dx = 0: if the wavefunction starts normalized, it stays normalized forever. Probability is neither created nor destroyed.

核心操作Core Operation

量子理论中最重要的操作之一：概率振幅 → 概率，靠的是取模方。One of the core operations in quantum theory is turning probability amplitudes into probabilities by taking the modulus squared.

时间无关势能Time-Independent Potential

定态：分离变量得到常微分方程Stationary States from Separation of Variables

\psi(x,t)=\phi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}

如果势能 V(x) 不显含时间，我们把时间部分单独写成一个相位因子。If the potential V(x) is time-independent, the time dependence can be isolated as a phase factor.

-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2\phi}{dx^2} + V(x)\phi(x) = E\phi(x)

这就是定态薛定谔方程，一个关于 \phi(x) 的常微分方程。This is the time-independent Schrödinger equation, an ordinary differential equation for \phi(x).

因为 |\psi|^2 = |\phi(x)|^2 不随时间变化，所以称为“定态”。Because |\psi|^2 = |\phi(x)|^2 does not change in time, this is called a stationary state.

\psi(x,t)=\sum_n c_n \phi_n(x)e^{-iE_n t/\hbar}

第一个例子First Example

无限深势阱：把粒子关进盒子里The Infinite Square Well: A Particle in a Box

势能设定Potential Setup

V(x)=0 \;(0<x<L), \qquad V(x)=\infty \;(\text{outside})

  • 粒子只能存在于 0<x<L 内。The particle can exist only in 0<x<L.
  • 边界条件：\phi(0)=\phi(L)=0Boundary conditions: \phi(0)=\phi(L)=0.
  • 这和固定两端的弦的驻波问题非常相似。This is closely analogous to standing waves on a string fixed at both ends.

求解Solving It

边界条件使能量离散化Boundary Conditions Discretize the Energy

\frac{d^2\phi}{dx^2} = -\frac{2mE}{\hbar^2}\phi

令 k^2 = 2mE/\hbar^2，则通解为Let k^2 = 2mE/\hbar^2. Then the general solution is

\phi(x) = A\sin(kx)+B\cos(kx)

由 \phi(0)=0 得 B=0，由 \phi(L)=0 得 \sin(kL)=0。From \phi(0)=0, we get B=0. From \phi(L)=0, we get \sin(kL)=0.

kL=n\pi,\qquad n=1,2,3,\dots

E_n=\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2mL^2}

\phi_n(x)=\sqrt{\frac{2}{L}}\sin\frac{n\pi x}{L}

量子化是方程 + 边界条件的自然结果，不需要额外人为加入。Quantization is the natural result of the equation plus the boundary conditions. No extra postulate is needed.

可视化Visualization

前三个本征态与节点结构The First Three Eigenstates and Their Nodes

结论Takeaway

节点越多，能量越高。量子化就像固定两端弦的驻波频率，只能取特定值。More nodes mean higher energy. Quantization emerges just like the allowed standing-wave frequencies of a string fixed at both ends.

回扣历史Back to the History

我们现在知道量子化从哪里来Now We See Where Quantization Comes From

Bohr 的离散能级Bohr's Discrete Levels

在第一讲里它还是经验性假设；现在我们看到，离散能级可以从波动方程和边界条件中自然出现。In Lecture 1 this was still an empirical postulate. Now we see that discrete levels can emerge naturally from a wave equation plus boundary conditions.

Planck 的量子假设Planck's Quantum Hypothesis

能量差对应定态之间的跃迁，量子化不再只是神秘规则，而有了波动性的根源。Energy gaps correspond to transitions between stationary states, so quantization is no longer just a mysterious rule.

一句物理图像One Physical Picture

量子化 = 波动性 + 约束条件。就像管风琴只能发出某些音高，束缚粒子也只能占据某些能级。Quantization = wave character + constraints. Just as an organ pipe supports only certain notes, a bound particle supports only certain energies.

数学结构Mathematical Structure

波函数的基本性质Basic Properties of Wavefunctions

连续性Continuity

在有限势能处，\phi 与 d\phi/dx 必须连续。For finite potentials, both \phi and d\phi/dx must be continuous.

归一化Normalization

\int |\phi|^2 dx = 1 表示总概率为一。\int |\phi|^2 dx = 1 means total probability equals one.

正交性Orthogonality

\int \phi_m^* \phi_n dx = \delta_{mn}。\int \phi_m^* \phi_n dx = \delta_{mn}.

完备性Completeness

任意态都可以展开成定态的叠加，这将在后续 Dirac 符号中变得更简洁。Any state can be expanded as a superposition of stationary states, which will become cleaner in Dirac notation later.

动态行为Dynamics

叠加态会随时间振荡：量子拍A Superposition Oscillates in Time: Quantum Beats

\psi(x,0)=c_1\phi_1 + c_2\phi_2

\psi(x,t)=c_1\phi_1 e^{-iE_1 t/\hbar}+c_2\phi_2 e^{-iE_2 t/\hbar}

时间依赖来自哪里？Where Does the Time Dependence Come From?

不同能量分量积累不同相位，因此概率密度会以 (E_2-E_1)/\hbar 的频率振荡。Different energy components accumulate different phases, so the probability density oscillates with frequency (E_2-E_1)/\hbar.

结论Takeaway

定态不动，叠加态会动。时间演化来自相位差，而不是经典力学中的轨道。Stationary states stay still; superpositions move. Time evolution comes from relative phase, not classical trajectories.

连接实验Connecting to Experiment

期望值：如何从波函数得到可观测量Expectation Values: How to Get Observables from the Wavefunction

位置、动量与一般算符Position, Momentum, and General Operators

\langle x \rangle = \int \psi^* x \psi \, dx

\langle p \rangle = \int \psi^* \left(-i\hbar \frac{d}{dx}\right)\psi \, dx

为什么 \hat{p} = -i\hbar\frac{d}{dx}？回忆第 6 页：空间导数从平面波中提取动量，-i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial x}e^{ikx}=\hbar k\, e^{ikx}=p\,e^{ikx}。Why \hat{p} = -i\hbar\frac{d}{dx}? Recall Slide 6: the spatial derivative extracts momentum from a plane wave, -i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial x}e^{ikx}=\hbar k\, e^{ikx}=p\,e^{ikx}.

\langle A \rangle = \int \psi^* \hat{A} \psi \, dx

势阱定态中的例子Examples in the Infinite Well

  • \langle x \rangle = L/2：由对称性决定。\langle x \rangle = L/2 by symmetry.
  • \langle p \rangle = 0：驻波没有净动量。\langle p \rangle = 0 because a standing wave carries no net momentum.
提醒Reminder

期望值是大量重复实验的统计平均，不是“粒子实际在的地方”。An expectation value is a statistical average over repeated measurements, not “where the particle actually is.”
