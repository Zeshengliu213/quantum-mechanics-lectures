# Quantum Mechanics for Organic Electronics

**A 12-Lecture Series**

Interactive lecture slides for a graduate-level quantum mechanics course, with a focus on organic semiconductor physics.

*Zesheng Liu · Linköping University · Laboratory of Organic Electronics*

> **Understanding over derivation. History over formalism. Animation over equation.**

## Features

- Browser-based slide deck with keyboard/touch navigation
- Bilingual support (中文/English) with language toggle
- Interactive canvas animations and visualizations
- KaTeX math rendering
- Fullscreen support (press `F`)

### Lecture 01 Animations
- Blackbody radiation spectrum (adjustable temperature)
- Photoelectric effect (frequency & intensity controls)
- Double-slit electron interference (real-time accumulation)
- Compton scattering (adjustable angle)
- Stern-Gerlach experiment (single & cascaded)
- Uncertainty principle visualization

### Lecture 02 Animations
- Classical trajectory vs. double-slit electron interference
- Uncertainty principle (position–momentum Gaussian pair)
- Two-state superposition interference

### Lecture 03 Animations
- Adjustable quantum number eigenstate viewer (ψₙ and |ψₙ|²)
- HOMO-LUMO gap vs. box length (interactive slider)
- Finite vs. infinite well wave function comparison
- Acene series absorption data visualization

### Lecture 04 Animations
- Classical vs. quantum oscillator probability density
- Harmonic oscillator eigenstates with Hermite polynomials
- Ladder operator step-by-step derivation
- Interactive Huang-Rhys factor S and vibronic spectrum
- Franck-Condon overlap visualization

### Lecture 05 Animations
- Tunneling through a rectangular barrier
- Transmission coefficient vs. barrier parameters
